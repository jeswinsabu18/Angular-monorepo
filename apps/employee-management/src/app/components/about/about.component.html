Privacy and security

  • Do not enter sensitive or confidential personal information.
  • Passwords are saved unencrypted in this browser. Do not reuse a password that you use anywhere else.
  • Log out when you finish, particularly on a shared computer.
  • Permission checks happen in the browser, so they are a convenience for this prototype, not a substitute for server-side security.

Disclaimer

This application is intended for demonstration, learning, and internal testing. Information shown may be sample data and should be verified before it is used for operational decisions.

It should not be treated as a secure production system for confidential employee information without server-side security, authorisation, backups, and data protection measures.

The application is provided as is, without a guarantee that it will be error-free or suitable for every business requirement.
